How to Use

The 5-second habit

Money flows → you log it. That's the whole system. Open GYST, pick the type, amount, account, category, done.

Flow Types

OUT (−) — Money leaving you. Groceries, rent, Netflix.
IN (+) — Money coming in. Salary, freelance, cashback.
TRANSFER — Money moving between your own accounts. UPI top-up, FD deposit.

Budget Groups

Survival — Groceries, Transport, Utilities, Health, Education. Non-negotiable spending.
Wealth — Investments. Money that works for you.
Wants — Dining, Relationships, Vice. Quality of life spending.
OneOff — Overheads. One-time or irregular expenses.
Nonspend — Transfers, Lending, Adjustments. Not real spending.

The Dashboard

Tap Dashboard → see your net worth, account balances, and spending breakdown. Use the Time Machine to check any past month.

Ghost Money

Your GYST balance and your bank app will diverge over time. That's normal. Use the Verify button in Accounts to find the gap, then log an Adjustment to reconcile.

FAQ

I forgot to log something. Is it ruined?

No. Log it now with the actual amount. GYST doesn't care when you logged it — only what you logged. The numbers will correct themselves.

My balance doesn't match my bank. What do I do?

Use Dashboard → Accounts → Verify. Enter your real balance. The difference is ghost money — log an Adjustment entry to reconcile.

What's the difference between Escrow / Lending and Transfer?

Escrow / Lending is money that left your account but isn't truly spent — it will (should) come back. Transfer is moving money between your own accounts — it's not an expense at all.

How do I add a new bank account?

Menu → Manage Accounts → Add the account → Save. It will appear everywhere automatically.

Can I edit a logged entry?

Not from the HUD yet. You can edit directly in the HUDLogs tab of your Google Sheet if absolutely needed. Don't touch any other tab.

What is Adjustment?

A correction entry. Use it to account for interest, cashback, rounding differences, or ghost money write-offs. It keeps your books clean without cluttering your real expense categories.

About

GYST — moneyFlow

A personal finance system built for imperfect humans.

Built with Google Sheets as the engine and a fast web app as the interface. No subscriptions. No servers. Your data lives in your own Google Sheet — private by design.

Philosophy

Most finance tools fail because they assume you are disciplined, consistent, and never forget anything. GYST assumes the opposite — and keeps working anyway.

The only discipline it requires: log transactions when money moves.
